Testing Standards

What our testing covers

Each batch of research peptides is tested before being stocked. Results are documented and retained. We do not stock products without purity verification.

🔬
HPLC Purity Analysis

High-Performance Liquid Chromatography is the industry standard for quantifying peptide purity. Each product batch is verified to ≥99% purity using this method.

📋
Certificate of Analysis

A COA documents the test methodology, batch number, test date, purity result and tester identity. Available on request for every product we stock.

🧬
Identity Verification

Mass spectrometry (MS) confirms molecular identity — that the compound is what it is stated to be. This runs alongside HPLC for complete verification.

💧
Water Content Testing

Karl Fischer titration is used to determine residual moisture in lyophilised peptides, ensuring product stability and accurate dosing for research applications.
