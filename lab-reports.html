Published Independent Reports

Two independent reports. Both 99% by HPLC.

We sent samples from our own UK stock to an independent analytical laboratory in Poland and paid for HPLC analysis. Every report we hold is published here in full and unedited - including the vial photographs the laboratory took.

Independently Tested

Retatrutide

Analiza Białek sp. z o.o. · Wrocław, Poland · Report 100016392

99%Purity (HPLC)

15mgLabel Claim
Jul 2026Analysed

A sealed 15mg retail vial from our UK stock was analysed by reverse-phase HPLC on a BioBasic 8 column against a water/acetonitrile gradient. Purity came back at 99%, with vial content measured at 16.49mg against a 15mg label claim - over the stated weight, not under. All Retatrutide vial sizes are filled from the same material.
