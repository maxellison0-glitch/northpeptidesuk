## Notes
- Tier 1 first: each maps to a flagship product and a query with clear buyer intent.
- The handling cluster (Tier 3) exists to interlink and funnel to consumables — keep
  every handling guide cross-linked to the reconstitution guide and to BAC water.
- Comparison and "what is X" formats tend to win featured snippets; favour them.
- Never write outcome, dosing, or therapeutic claims to chase volume — the gate will
  block them, and they are the exact claims UK MHRA/ASA enforce against.
