Six Pillars

What sets us apart

Every aspect of how we source, store, test and dispatch is designed for one purpose: supplying research-grade peptides you can trust.

01
Purity & Testing
HPLC Verified Per Batch

Every product is verified to ≥99% purity using High-Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC). This is not a blanket claim applied to the product line — it is a result from the specific batch in stock. Mass spectrometry (MS) confirms molecular identity alongside purity.

✓ HPLC purity documented for every batch
✓ MS identity verification alongside purity
✓ Certificate of Analysis available on request
✓ Batch documentation available on request →
02
UK Stock
UK Stocked

All stock is held in the UK, not shipped from overseas after ordering. This means no international customs delay, no extended transit at uncontrolled temperatures, and reliable dispatch timelines for UK researchers.

✓ No re-export from China or EU after order
✓ UK Customs already cleared on arrival
✓ Controlled transit from storage to you
03
Cold-Chain Handling
Frozen from Storage to Dispatch

Lyophilised peptides are temperature-sensitive. Our cold-chain protocol keeps every product frozen from the moment of arrival through to the point of dispatch packaging. Thermal integrity is not a policy statement — it is a handling procedure we follow on every order.

✓ Stored at −20°C from arrival
✓ Removed from cold storage only at dispatch
✓ Thermal packing used on all shipments
04
Storage Standards
−20°C Controlled Environment

Peptides in lyophilised form are stable over longer periods when stored correctly. We maintain −20°C cold storage and do not keep stock at ambient temperature. Batch rotation is managed to ensure freshness of supply relative to manufacture date.

✓ Dedicated −20°C freezer storage
✓ Batch rotation by manufacture date
✓ No room-temperature stocking
05
Dispatch Process
Within 24h of Confirmed Payment

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of confirmed payment, Monday to Friday. Shipping options include Royal Mail Tracked 24 and Special Delivery for time-sensitive research requirements. All orders are packed discretely with no external branding.

✓ 24h dispatch on confirmed payment (Mon–Fri)
✓ Royal Mail Tracked 24 or Special Delivery
✓ Discrete packaging, no external branding

End-to-End

From manufacturer to your door

Here is the complete chain of custody for every product we supply — from sourcing through to delivery.

01

Source Selection

Products are sourced exclusively from established manufacturers with documented quality management processes. We do not purchase from intermediary brokers or resellers without manufacturer traceability.

Established Manufacturers No Unverified Resellers
02

Batch Arrival & Verification

On arrival, each batch is logged with batch number, manufacture date and test documentation. COA data is recorded against the batch reference and retained for researcher requests.

Batch Number Logged COA Filed on Arrival HPLC Results Recorded
03

Frozen Storage

All stock is placed into −20°C cold storage immediately on arrival. Products do not sit at ambient temperature. Cold storage is monitored and batch rotation is managed by manufacture date to prioritise freshness.

−20°C Freezer No Ambient Staging Date-Rotated Stock
04

Order Picking & Packing

Products are removed from cold storage at the point of order picking only. Thermal packing is used on all shipments. Outer packaging is plain and discrete with no product identification externally.

Cold Storage to Pack Thermal Packaging Discrete Outer Box
05

Tracked UK Dispatch

Orders are dispatched within 24 hours of confirmed payment (Monday to Friday). Royal Mail Tracked 24 or Special Delivery options are available. Tracking details are provided at dispatch.

Royal Mail Tracked 24 Special Delivery Available 24h Dispatch
