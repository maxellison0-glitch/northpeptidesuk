# Evidence

- GLP-1 delayed gastric emptying in a controlled human study: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/11841992/

- GHRH and sleep-related growth-hormone pulses in humans: https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/2005213/
