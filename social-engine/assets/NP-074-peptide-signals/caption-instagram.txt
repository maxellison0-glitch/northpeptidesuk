What can peptides actually do?

Not one vague promise — different peptide signals, different receptors, different outputs:

• Insulin → GLUT4 → glucose uptake
• GLP‑1 → incretin signalling, insulin up, glucagon down, slower gastric emptying
• GHK‑Cu → fibroblast and extracellular-matrix research
• Vasopressin → V2 receptor → aquaporin‑2 → water retention
• GHRH → pituitary pulse → GH / IGF‑1 axis
