# NP-075 evidence notes

- Retatrutide: Jastreboff et al., NEJM phase 2 trial. The 12 mg group had a least-squares mean body-weight change of −24.2% at week 48 versus −2.1% with placebo. This is a controlled trial in adults with obesity; it is not a universal result or a recommendation.
- Semaglutide: SELECT trial. Major adverse cardiovascular event composite was 6.5% vs 8.0% with placebo (HR 0.80) in adults with overweight/obesity and established cardiovascular disease without diabetes.
- GHK-Cu age range: review literature reports plasma GHK around 200 ng/mL at age 20–25 and around 80 ng/mL at age 60–80. This is a reported observational range, not proof that supplementation restores youthful physiology.
- GHK-Cu wound model: Maquart et al. reported concentration-dependent increases in dry weight, DNA, total protein, collagen and glycosaminoglycans in rat wound chambers.
- GHRH/GH: normal physiology studies associate the major early-night GH pulse with slow-wave sleep; GH output also declines with age. This does not establish human age reversal.

Intentionally excluded: cancer-prevention language, “brain reversal,” and systemic injectable anti-ageing promises. The retrieved evidence does not support those as human outcome claims.
