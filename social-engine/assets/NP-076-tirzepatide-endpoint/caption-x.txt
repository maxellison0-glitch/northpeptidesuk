20.9% is the number everyone quotes.

SURMOUNT-1 reported −20.9% mean body-weight change at week 72 in the 15 mg tirzepatide group; placebo was −3.1%.

The context: 20-week dose escalation, then a 72-week endpoint. Not a four-week hack. Not a promise for every person.

Research context only. Not medical advice. For laboratory research use only. Not for human consumption.
