20.9% is the number everyone quotes. Here is what it actually was:

In SURMOUNT-1, a phase 3 randomised trial of 2,539 adults with obesity or overweight plus a weight-related condition (without diabetes), the 15 mg tirzepatide group had a mean body-weight change of −20.9% at week 72. The placebo group was −3.1% in the same treatment-regimen analysis.

The part social media usually skips: this was a 72-week endpoint, with a 20-week dose-escalation period. It is not a four-week hack, a before/after promise, or a prediction for every person.

The number is real. The context is the point.
