# Evidence notes — NP-076

Primary source: Jastreboff AM et al. Tirzepatide Once Weekly for the Treatment of Obesity. *New England Journal of Medicine* (2022). https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038

- SURMOUNT-1 was a phase 3, double-blind, randomised, placebo-controlled trial.
- It enrolled 2,539 adults with BMI ≥30, or BMI ≥27 plus at least one weight-related complication, excluding diabetes.
- The trial ran for 72 weeks, including a 20-week dose-escalation period.
- The 15 mg group had a −20.9% mean body-weight change at week 72 in the treatment-regimen estimand; placebo was −3.1%.
- This is a trial-level mean in a defined population, not an individual forecast.
- The creative deliberately avoids dosage instructions, treatment recommendations, guaranteed outcomes, or claims about body composition beyond the reported endpoint.
