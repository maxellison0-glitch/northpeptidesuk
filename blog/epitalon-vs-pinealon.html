What Each One Is

Epitalon is a synthetic tetrapeptide associated with the pineal gland. It is studied in peptide-bioregulator research and in cellular-aging research models.

Pinealon is a synthetic tripeptide in the short-peptide "bioregulator" family, often referred to as a brain peptide. It is studied in cellular and neuropeptide research models.

Both are supplied purely as reference research compounds. North Peptides UK makes no claims about effects in humans, and nothing here is guidance for human or animal use.

The Core Difference

Both are short peptide bioregulators associated with the pineal gland, but they differ in length and research focus — Epitalon is a four-amino-acid peptide most associated with cellular-aging research, while Pinealon is a three-amino-acid peptide studied more in neuropeptide and cellular research models. They are sometimes studied alongside one another within the same bioregulator research context.

Handling and Reconstitution

Both reconstitute the same way, in bacteriostatic water. Choose the solvent volume that gives the concentration your work requires, add it down the side of the vial, swirl gently, and refrigerate the prepared solution. Our reconstitution guide covers the concentration arithmetic.
