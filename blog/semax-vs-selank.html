What Each One Is

Semax is a synthetic peptide based on a short fragment of the ACTH (adrenocorticotropic hormone) sequence. In research literature it is described as a nootropic peptide and is studied in cognition and neuroprotection research models.

Selank is a synthetic peptide based on the immunopeptide tuftsin. It is described in research literature as an anxiolytic nootropic peptide and is studied in research models relating to anxiety-like behaviour and neuropeptide signalling.

Both are supplied purely as reference research compounds. North Peptides UK makes no claims about effects in humans, and nothing here is guidance for human or animal use.

The Core Difference

The main distinction is their origin and research focus. Semax derives from an ACTH fragment and is most associated with cognition and neuroprotection research; Selank derives from tuftsin and is most associated with anxiety-related and immunomodulation research. Some research programmes hold both to compare the two peptide families side by side.
