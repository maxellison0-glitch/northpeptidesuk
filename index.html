    Quick Answers

    Not sure where to start?

    What are the main stocked options?

    The main stocked choices are Retatrutide, Tirzepatide and KLOW Stack. Retatrutide and Tirzepatide sit in the metabolic research category, while KLOW Stack is a combined blend option for researchers who want GHK-Cu, TB-500, BPC-157 and KPV in one vial.
    Start with the catalogue above

    Which product should I look at first?

    If you are comparing the metabolic research options, start by looking at Retatrutide and Tirzepatide side by side. If you want a combined vial rather than separate repair-focused products, look at KLOW Stack.
    Compare Retatrutide, Tirzepatide and KLOW

    Do I need the checkout basics?

    The checkout basics are optional. They are there so you can add 10ml bac water, a syringe kit and alcohol wipes in the same order if needed. They pair most naturally with vial orders such as Retatrutide, Tirzepatide or KLOW Stack.
    Add basics at checkout only if useful

    How does payment and dispatch work?

    Add your chosen product, review your basket and continue to Stripe checkout. Once payment is complete, your order is packed for UK dispatch. The most common routes are simple: choose Retatrutide, Tirzepatide or KLOW Stack, then checkout.
