The Analysis

What was tested, and by whom

We sent a sealed 15mg vial of our Retatrutide to Analiza Białek sp. z o.o., an independent analytical laboratory based at ul. Duńska 9, 54-427 Wrocław, Poland. The laboratory has no commercial relationship with our suppliers. We paid for the analysis and had no involvement in carrying it out.

The sample was analysed by reverse-phase HPLC. A 3µl injection was applied to a BioBasic 8 (Thermo Scientific) column and run against a linear water/acetonitrile gradient with 0.1% trifluoroacetic acid. The resulting peak was compared against a reference chromatogram for Retatrutide.

Reading The Result

What this report does and does not cover

The purity figure of 99% describes the proportion of the analysed sample identified as Retatrutide against the reference chromatogram. The content figure of 16.49mg is the mass of Retatrutide measured in the vial, against a stated label claim of 15mg — the vial contained slightly more than the label states, not less.

Scope and limitations

This report describes the specific sample submitted to the laboratory. It is strong evidence for the stock it was drawn from, but it is not a per-vial certificate and it does not carry forward automatically to future stock. We will retest and publish again when stock is replaced.

The analysis covers identity, purity and content only. It is not a sterility, endotoxin or heavy-metals test, and it is not a statement of safety, efficacy or suitability for use in humans or animals. All products remain strictly for laboratory research use only.
