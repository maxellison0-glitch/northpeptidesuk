How consent works

On your first visit, the site offers Accept analytics and Reject non-essential. TikTok Pixel and configured Google Analytics or Google Tag Manager scripts do not load before you accept. Rejection keeps them blocked. Your choice is stored in your browser so the site can respect it on later visits.

You can change or withdraw your choice at any time. Withdrawal does not make earlier consent-based processing unlawful.

Providers and transfers

Consent-based information may be sent to TikTok and Google. These providers may process data outside the UK using the safeguards described in our Privacy Notice. Their own retention periods and controls may also apply.

Browser controls

You can also delete or block cookies and local storage using browser settings. Blocking necessary storage may stop the basket or consent preference from being retained. Browser deletion does not by itself erase data already received by an analytics provider; use the rights and contact routes in our Privacy Notice where applicable.
