# Storefront refinement — September 2026

## Brief

Keep cookie controls out of the shopping journey after a choice. Replace the repeated pill-shaped boxes with a coherent visual treatment. Give catalogue photographs frames that fit them properly.

## Direction

- Palette: North navy #0E1F38, action blue #1F6FEB, white #FFFFFF, frost #F5F9FC, border #D5E2EE, verified text #0A7A54.
- Type: retain the existing Space Grotesk headings, Inter body/control text and Syne wordmark. Reserve mono for compact product data; reduce tracked capitals in controls.
- Layout: consistent square photography above product name, size, price and action. Quiet cards, plain metadata, small corners on controls. Keep laboratory reports clearly linked.
- Interaction: familiar menus and accordions, visible focus, 44px tap targets. Cookie choices stay explicit; settings live in the footer. Existing checkout extras remain collapsed by default.
- Restraint: the existing product photographs carry the distinctive material. Remove duplicate trust pills, floating cookie chrome, oversized rounding and decorative shadows; retain brand identity and genuine content.
